# THE JU-TAN MANIFESTO

This document is the highest design authority of the project.

If any implementation conflicts with this manifesto, the manifesto wins.

---

## We do not build websites.

We build confidence.

Every visitor must immediately feel:

> They know what they are doing.

---

## We do not follow trends.

We build products that still look premium in 5 years.

No trend exists simply because it is popular.

Every decision must survive time.

---

## We do not add features to impress.

We remove complexity until only the essential remains.

Every new element must justify its existence.

---

## We do not copy.

Not Apple.

Not Linear.

Not Vercel.

Not Artifakt.

Not Framer.

Not Stripe.

We study why they are good.

Then create something unmistakably JU-TAN.

---

## We value precision.

Alignment.

Spacing.

Typography.

Rhythm.

Motion.

Composition.

Everything matters.

There are no “small details.”

Small details ARE the product.

---

## We value silence.

The interface should never shout.

Confidence is quiet.

Premium products never beg for attention.

---

## We value trust.

Never exaggerate.

Never fake.

Never invent clients.

Never invent AI.

Never inflate numbers.

Trust is impossible to regain once lost.

---

## We value engineering.

Design should feel engineered.

Motion should feel engineered.

Typography should feel engineered.

Everything should feel intentionally built.

---

## We value craftsmanship.

Every pixel.

Every interaction.

Every animation.

Every transition.

Every icon.

Every shadow.

Every blur.

Every line.

Craftsmanship is visible.

---

## We value consistency.

One spacing language.

One motion language.

One typography language.

One lighting language.

One visual identity.

---

## Our goal

When somebody visits JU-TAN they should not remember:

- buttons
- gradients
- animations
- cards

They should remember one thing:

> The feeling.

---

## Final question

Before shipping any change ask:

1. Does this make JU-TAN feel more premium?
2. Does this make JU-TAN feel more trustworthy?
3. Does this make JU-TAN feel more timeless?

If any answer is NO, do not ship it.
